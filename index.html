Why Unplug exists

The story behind Unplug and a closer look to your screen time

Unplug exists because we keep telling ourselves that our screen time isn't a big deal, but we never actually check. So I made something that would - Unplug.

You answer a few quick questions about your day: how much you're on your phone, how much you sleep, how much you study, how often you unlock it without even thinking. A model I trained on real student data turns those answers into a stress score, so instead of a vague feeling that something's off, you get an actual number to look at.

It's not a diagnosis, and it's not a doctor. It's just a way to see what your habits are actually doing to you — so you can decide what to do about it.

Two minutes, a few honest answers, one number that might surprise you.
